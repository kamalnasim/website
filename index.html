A life, told in songs.

I'm Nasim Shiplu Kamal, an independent Bengali singer and songwriter. This website brings together my original music, the ideas behind it, and the personal thoughts that travel alongside the songs.

Some days it will be about a new release; other days, a memory, a poem, or something ordinary that made me stop and think. This is a work in progress—just like the journey itself.
