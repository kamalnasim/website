Poetry & fragments

Words that need a place to live.

Some thoughts arrive as songs. Others arrive as lines that refuse to be sung too quickly. This is where I keep those pieces — the unfinished, the honest, and the small but lasting things that words sometimes hold.

A poem from the archive

জানালা

A poem about looking out, looking back, and the life held in familiar corners.

Some windows look out into the world. Others quietly open into the rooms we carry within ourselves.

Poetry, like music, gives certain feelings a place to stay when ordinary conversation is not enough.
