A little about me

The person behind the songs.

This is not a formal press bio. It is an introduction to the person whose music and writing live here.

Nasim Shiplu Kamal
Singer · Songwriter · Story collector

Hello, I'm Nasim.

I'm an independent Bengali singer and songwriter. I created this website as a home for my original music, personal writing, poetry, and the stories behind them.

I believe a song is more than a recording. It carries the people, moments, places, and feelings that helped it come into being. I wanted a place where those stories could live alongside the music.

You'll find Bengali songs here, thoughts in Bengali and English, and notes from the ongoing process of making things. This site will change as I do.

This is a first-pass biography. It intentionally avoids inventing personal history. You can later replace it with your own preferred introduction.
