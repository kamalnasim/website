A personal introduction

Not a press biography. Just a little about why this space exists.

Hello, I'm Nasim.
Welcome to my world.

I was born in Noakhali, Bangladesh and now live in Sydney, Australia. Much of my life has unfolded between those two places, carrying different languages, memories and ways of seeing the world.

By profession, I work in technology. It is a world built on structure, logic and precision. Music comes from a different place entirely. Most of the songs I write begin with a memory, a feeling, a conversation or something I cannot quite explain until it finds its way into a melody. It became a way to stay connected to language, memory and the places that shaped me.

Most of my songs are written in Bengali and inspired by relationships, nostalgia, migration, family and the quiet moments that often go unnoticed. Over time, I realised that engineering and songwriting are not as different as they seem. Both begin with curiosity. Both search for patterns. Both are ways of making sense of the world.

I also enjoy writing — collecting thoughts, revisiting memories, and paying attention to the small details of everyday life. A passing conversation, a familiar place, a feeling that refuses to leave: sometimes these become the beginning of a song or a piece of writing.

This website is my attempt to bring those things together. It's a home for my original Bengali music, poetry, personal reflections, and the stories behind what I create.

Not everything here is polished or finished. Some things are simply worth keeping, and some are worth sharing. All of them are honest.
